
# Northstar Enterprise Services - IT Operations Guide

This guide contains fictional IT support policies and procedures for classroom use.

## Laptop Replacement Policy

Employees can request a laptop replacement if their device is damaged, outdated or no longer suitable for their work.

IT Support must inspect the device before approving a replacement.

Devices under warranty should be referred to the hardware support team.

Replacement requests require manager approval and asset verification.

## Incident Priority Guide

IT incidents are classified into three priority levels.

High priority incidents affect critical business operations, multiple employees or important business systems.

Medium priority incidents affect an employee's ability to work but have an available workaround.

Low priority incidents involve minor issues that do not significantly affect business operations.

The IT support team assigns priority based on business impact and urgency.

## VPN Troubleshooting

Employees experiencing VPN connection problems should first check their internet connection.

Restarting the VPN application and reconnecting may resolve temporary connection issues.

Employees should verify that they are using their company credentials.

If the problem continues, they should contact Network Support and provide the error message.

Employees must never share their passwords with support personnel.

## Lost or Stolen Device Procedure

Employees must immediately report a lost or stolen company device to IT Support and their manager.

IT Support should record the incident and notify the appropriate security team.

The organization may remotely lock or wipe the device to protect company information.

Employees should provide the asset tag and last known location if available.

## Software Installation Rules

Employees must use approved software from the organization's authorized software catalog.

Administrative approval may be required before installing new software.

Employees must not install pirated, unlicensed or unauthorized applications.

Software installation requests should be submitted to IT Support for review.

## Ticket Escalation Process

Employees should first report technical issues through the IT service desk.

The assigned support team investigates the issue and records troubleshooting steps.

If the issue cannot be resolved within the team's responsibility, it should be escalated to the appropriate support team.

High priority incidents affecting critical business operations should be escalated immediately.

Ticket updates should include the current status, actions taken and any pending information.

## Unavailable Policy Information

This guide does not specify the reimbursement amount for employees using personal internet connections.

Employees should contact HR or the appropriate company department for reimbursement-related questions.
